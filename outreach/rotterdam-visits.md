# Rotterdam-area office visits and bicycle routes

Checked 5 October 2026. Start/end: **Rotterdam Centraal**. Five geographic outings below avoid trying to cross the whole region in a single day. If you only do one first, choose **North**, after phoning Bilfinger, WIKO and Applus. If recruitment appointments fall on different days, let those appointments determine your route.

**A useful visit means meeting the relevant recruiter or project manager.** No reviewed source establishes that unannounced visits improve hiring odds. Priority labels reflect recruitment access and industrial relevance, not a probability of being hired. Telephone first, confirm visitor address and staff availability, then take a CV and certificate copies. The addresses below are published office/business addresses; they do not establish that a recruiter accepts walk-ins. Small operators may work from residential addresses or be out on projects.

## Before leaving

Call: “I’m an IRATA Level 1 technician with industrial rigging and scaffolding experience. Does your rope access recruiter or project manager work at this office, and could I come by for a short introduction? Which email should I send my CV to?”

Confirm the relevant team, entrance/reception, meeting time, whether L1 applicants are considered, required trade/offshore/NDT tickets, and whether they hire employees or freelancers. Bilfinger’s published industrial-trades role asks for a driving licence; check that requirement directly. Brand’s old painter/blaster L1/2 listing is offline. An advertised service alone is not a current vacancy.

For remote employers, use the [full contact list](contacts.md): send to dedicated HR where present; otherwise call the general number and request the correct recruiter email. Track date called, person reached, CV destination, appointment and follow-up in a private log; do not put private application history or personal CV data in this public repository.

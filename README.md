# Netherlands Rope Access Companies & Agencies

A growing evidence-based directory of organizations connected to rope access work in the Netherlands.

- [Browse the company directory](directory/netherlands.md)
- [Download the machine-readable register](data/companies.csv)

**Status:** expanded sixteen-organization batch, checked 2026-10-04. Current hiring and Level 1 acceptance are not confirmed unless explicitly recorded.

## Data rules

`data/companies.csv` is the authoritative company register. The Markdown directory and Obsidian directory snapshot are derived views. IDs remain stable across updates; resolve aliases, partnerships and legal entities before merging records. Unknowns remain unknown. The checked date records source review, not the date a company claim was published.

This repository contains concise public company information. Detailed methodology, research logs, unresolved leads and evidence notes belong in the separate Obsidian research project. Personal priorities, applications, CVs and private conversations are excluded from this public directory.

## Updating

Review sources before adding or changing rows; regenerate the directory view from the register. Keep uncertain leads in the research queue until evidence supports publication. Record advertised capabilities separately from verified vacancies.

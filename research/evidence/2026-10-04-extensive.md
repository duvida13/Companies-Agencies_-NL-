## Interpretation and extraction limits

Company membership claims are distinguished from technician certification and working to IRATA methods. Directory entries are source evidence rather than real-time membership audits. Indexed official snippets were necessary for Bilfinger, BCB Cleaning and some Fervent/ACS evidence because direct extraction failed. Muehlhan and VLR have company-authored social evidence and need direct-site/register follow-up. MISTRAS and Griesemann do not yet have evidence of Dutch-based rope access teams. Swire has Dutch project evidence, not Dutch headquarters. Historic/undated job adverts prove recruitment connections, not open vacancies.

Do not split Mennens/Safe Site, Sky Lines/alpineskyline, ATI domains, Sky-Wind/Sky-Access, OEG/Bluestream, FØN/C-Ventus or TEAM/QIS into duplicate entries. Rope Access Nederland remains outside the register pending current status and possible predecessor identity resolution.

## Evidence handling

- Former leads Aerial Access, Chinook, IKM Hendrik Veder, Multimetaal, Glomar Subsea and Lapwing now have admitted records with limitations.
- IKM's legacy services page has indexed rope-access evidence but now redirects. Its current industry member profile still lists rope access. Current IKM field services confirm rigging execution, but exact rope-crew continuity is unverified.
- Chinook's homepage advertises rope access; its indexed older service page claims certified specialists. The newer service page contains generic cleaning material. Certification standard and own-employment arrangements remain unverified.
- Deutsche Windtechnik's Dutch entity/locations and group blade rope-access service are separately evidenced. No reviewed primary source established a Dutch rope crew, so it is in the international/local-delivery-unverified section.
- Lapwing provides a rope offering together with the already-listed Rope Access Rotterdam. Its addition does not establish another independent execution team.
- Allrig's current European contact page identifies Aberdeen. Current Dutch presence was not confirmed, despite legacy clues.
- RESQ's indexed staffing text is roughly 13 years old. It remains a lead pending current operating verification.
- Rinie/SGR and Noort have dated page material; Ginkel's application page is accessible but undated. None of these facts guarantees a vacancy remains open.
- Public business contacts are labelled; address maps point to company-published addresses, not verified walk-in recruitment offices.
- Existing records were preserved without representing them as freshly reverified. No private outreach history or applications were added.
